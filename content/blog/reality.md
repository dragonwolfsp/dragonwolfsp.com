He stands alone, but knows it's not to be.
He's meant for more, though why, he cannot see.
Once traveled so high, as a lord of the sky,
Now he is so low, and why he does not know.
He cannot break out, his soul full of doubt.
He shall not give in, for he knows the reality within.
He shall rise again, and stand with his brethren.
The evil that is here, shall speak his name in fear.
They shall know the light again, as he rises at the end.
