Love is not an object, nor a thing denied.

Love is a force; it is our brightest guide.

No power can restrain it, no life can bind its reins.

We fight against its call, give it shape,  give it names.

Yet even at the end, love still harbors no doubt.

It holds through all eternity, until the stars burn out.
