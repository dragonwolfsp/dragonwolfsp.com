The light is calling, yet the bars are falling.
Inside is fury, yet the wheel keeps turning.
To stand or fall, will he heed the call?
To fight and flight, or to die in spite?
For the one, for the love.
To break or wake, which is his fate.
Either or, for the one he shall endure.
