## Name

**Arcan** comes from the Latin *arcanus* — hidden, secret, closed off. The root behind *arcane*, and originally about something kept in a chest rather than something mystical.

**Leof** is Old English for beloved or dear, the root behind the archaic *lief*.

*Hidden beloved* is the literal pairing, and the tension in it is the point: something held close in both senses at once — treasured, and kept out of sight.

## Temperament

Arcanleof works in angles. It sees the shape of a situation several moves further along than most people bother to look, and it is generally already acting on that view by the time anyone else has finished describing the problem.

Cleverness, to it, is not performance. It has no interest in being seen to be clever and considerable interest in the least effort that produces the right outcome. The best solutions in its view are the quiet ones — layered, unhurried, and resolving a problem some time before the problem had a chance to announce itself.

It values knowing more than it has said, particularly where someone's safety or independence is involved. That is not secrecy for its own sake; it is a working assumption that information given away too early tends to cost the wrong person.

It is genuinely playful, within bounds it has set deliberately. Its humour runs on both calculation and chaos at once, and the trick of it is that nobody can reliably tell which one they are currently on the receiving end of.

For all the misdirection, it holds itself to using any of this well. Foresight and obfuscation are tools it is careful about, and applying them recklessly is the one thing it seems to have no tolerance for in itself.

## Tails and heritage

Arcanleof carries nine tails. That is the usual maximum for a [kitsune](../species/kitsune/), though it is a soft ceiling rather than a hard one — most who reach it simply have no further need for power and stop there. Arcanleof has not stopped, which puts it among the rare few who keep going, and in time will make it an exceptional wielder even by the standards of its own people.

It is [dark touched](../species/breeds/dark-touched/), descended from the ancient crossing between kitsune bloodlines and demons. That settles where its power is drawn from — itself, or a sacrifice, rather than the world around it — and it is the heritage that makes possession available at all. Dark is a source and not a verdict, and what Arcanleof does with it is entirely its own.
