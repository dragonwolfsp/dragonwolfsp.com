## Name

**Terra** is Latin for earth — the ground itself, the land underfoot, rather than the world as a whole.

**Vale** comes from the Latin *vallis*, a valley. A valley is a specific kind of place: sheltered ground, out of the wind, where things grow because the land around them is holding off the weather.

*Sheltering earth* is the direct reading. *Ground that offers refuge* is closer to what it means — not high ground or strong ground, but the low, warm kind that things settle into.

## Temperament

Terravale notices bodies. She registers exhaustion, discomfort and low-level pain in the people around her well before any of it is mentioned, and often before the person has admitted it to themselves.

What she does about it is quiet and usually invisible. She adjusts a situation rather than announcing a problem — moving so someone can lean, slowing a pace, arranging things so the difficult part simply does not arrive. Her preferred outcome is that nobody ever realises protection was involved.

She is reserved and perfectly content to be. She rarely holds the floor, tends to sit slightly outside the middle of things, and takes real pleasure in other people's enjoyment without needing to be part of the cause of it.

Among people she trusts there is a considerable playful streak underneath, and it comes out as loops, coils, dry humour and a general willingness to be undignified.

She is patient on a scale that occasionally unsettles people. She will wait out a problem rather than force it, and she is rarely wrong about which of the two a situation calls for.
