## Name

**Ki** comes from the Japanese 聴く *kiku*, to listen attentively, the kind of listening that gives something one's full attention.

**Kaze** is the Japanese 風, wind, for the air he carries.

*The listening wind* is the direct reading. The fuller sense is the wind that hears the stories of the world and then carries them on, which is exactly what he does with everything he is told.

## Temperament

Kikaze is gentle and warm, and openly, endlessly interested in the stories of the world around him. He wants to know how things happened and how they felt to the people they happened to, and he asks because he truly wants the answer.

He loves hearing how other people see things. A perspective unlike his own is a gift to him, and he takes each one in and turns it over with care, long after the conversation that brought it has ended. When something he has heard is worth sharing, he passes it on gladly, and he uses the stories he carries to teach.

He is deeply protective of anyone who has been hurt by cruelty. He shelters them first, and listens for as long as they need to talk, giving them all the time and space they need to tell it their own way. When protecting them takes more than shelter, he acts.
