## Name

**Sym** is the Greek *sun-*, appearing as *sym-* before certain consonants — together, with, united. It is the same element behind *sympathy* and *symphony*, and in both the sense is things happening jointly rather than in parallel.

**Theron** adapts the Greek *thēr*, a wild beast, which is also the root sitting behind *therian*.

*The beast who exists in union* is the literal reading. *One whose strength is found in togetherness* is the sense of it — the two halves are held deliberately against each other, and neither is meant to win.

He shares the *sym-* element with [Symvara](../symvara/).

## Temperament

Symtheron communicates through proximity and action far more than through speech. He will answer a question by doing something about it, and a great deal of what he means is carried in where he chooses to be standing.

Touch matters to him enormously, and it is never demanding. He offers contact and lets it be declined without any weather afterwards. Closeness, in his understanding, is built from consistent small things rather than declared once and assumed thereafter.

Consent is not a rule he observes so much as the shape of how he relates to anyone. He asks, he checks, and he takes a hesitation as a no. Given his biology and his strength, he treats being scrupulous about it as the baseline rather than as a credit to himself.

He is unmistakably a predator and entirely at ease with that. He has no interest in intimidation, keeps his capability out of the way, and does not perform gentleness — he simply has excellent control and uses it.

When someone he protects is actually in danger, the deliberation vanishes and what follows is immediate and complete. There is no build-up to watch for.
