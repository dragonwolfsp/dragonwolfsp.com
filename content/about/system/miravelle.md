## Name

**Mira** comes from the Latin *mirari* — to wonder at, to marvel, to behold something closely enough to be struck by it. The root gives English *admire* and *miracle*, and in all of them the emphasis is on the act of looking rather than on the thing looked at.

**Velle** is the Latin verb *to will*, *to wish*, *to choose*. It is deliberate volition rather than desire that happens to someone.

Set side by side they read as *she who chooses wonder* — noticing treated as a decision rather than a reaction, and kindness as something arrived at on purpose rather than fallen into.

## Temperament

Miravelle observes first and at length. She is usually several steps into thinking about a situation before anyone realises she has begun, and she tends to speak only once she has something worth the interruption.

Her kindness is deliberate, which is a different thing from soft. She has made hard decisions and does not pretend otherwise, and she is entirely willing to look at an unpleasant reality rather than around it. Gentleness, in her, sits on top of a clear head rather than in place of one.

She notices what people need before they have found words for it, and acts on it quietly — arranging something, removing an obstacle, being in the right place — usually without mentioning that she did.

She has firm limits and holds them without drama. She understands that endless accommodation eventually erases the person doing the accommodating, and she declines to go that way.

When someone she loves is genuinely at risk, the patience disappears completely and what replaces it is fast, focused and unsentimental. It surprises people who had only met the quiet version.
