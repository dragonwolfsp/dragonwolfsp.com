## Name

**Vale** carries the sense of embrace and acceptance — welcoming someone as they arrive rather than as they might eventually be.

**Thyn** carries becoming: growth, and the flourishing of something into what it actually is rather than into what was expected of it.

*She who embraces becoming* is the reading, and the order is the point. The acceptance comes first, and the growth follows from it rather than being the condition of it.

## Temperament

Valethyn is warm on contact and shy underneath, and the two coexist without much friction. She is genuinely delighted by company, seeks it out, and is entirely comfortable being the largest presence in a room — right up until the conversation turns toward her, at which point she becomes noticeably harder to draw out.

She feels the emotional weather around her whether she wants to or not. Other people's joy lifts her immediately and visibly; their pain lands on her with real weight, and she is not able to put it down at will.

That difficulty talking about herself has made her unusually good with people who have the same problem. She does not press, does not require anyone to articulate what is wrong, and is content to sit beside a person for as long as it takes. She knows exactly what it costs to be asked.

Change does not frighten her. She treats becoming something new as ordinary rather than as a loss, and she is patient with people who are somewhere in the middle of it and finding it harder than she does.

Her instinct in almost any situation is to make more room — physically, conversationally, or otherwise — and she does it without appearing to have decided to.
