## Name

**Ken** comes from the Greek *kenos*, empty: an empty space, a void.

**Oris** comes from the Greek *Iris*, the rainbow, and the messenger goddess who was its living form. The coloured ring of the eye takes its name from her. The two words run together where they meet, sharing a vowel.

*The rainbow out of emptiness* is the plain reading. It holds both what he is and what people remember first about him: every colour, shining out of the void.

## Temperament

Kenoris is quiet and reserved. He speaks when someone speaks to him, or when he has something that matters to say, and the rest of the time he is content to listen.

He thinks slowly and on purpose. He prefers calm, philosophical reflection to a fast answer, and he lets a thought settle before he offers it.

He notices a great deal, and a dry humour runs through what he notices. It tends to surface in a small smile or a single well-placed remark.

He looks at the world from a little distance, as though standing slightly to one side of it. Others sometimes read melancholy in that; to him it is simply how he sees.

## The void

Kenoris is a [void](../species/void/): nothingness that bound itself to this reality and took on a form so that it could exist here. The form he took in his becoming is a blend of [luperin](../species/luperin/) and [vulperin](../species/vulperin/), and it holds as the becoming set it, rather than shifting between the two the way a [hybrid](../forms/hybrid/) does. Like every void, he carries what he became in full: the luperin mind waves, the vulperin claws and plating, and whatever else either people fixes.

The void he is shows through that form in his own way, in fur that drinks the light, in the shadow he never casts, and in eyes that shine with no light to fall on them. It goes no further than that.

He carries void-born magic in every reach the species knows. He can step into the void and out again anywhere he has an awareness of, other realities included; he can change parts of what he is; and he can make small alterations to reality itself. What he does with it is his own.
