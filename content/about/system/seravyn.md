## Name

**Sera** draws on Latin roots for serenity, calm and stillness — the settled kind that comes from having nothing to prove rather than from effort spent staying quiet.

**Vyn** draws on devotion, closeness and chosen bonds, the same element that closes several names here.

*The calm embrace* is the plainest reading, and *one who shelters through softness* is the fuller one. Both halves point the same way: steadiness offered outward.

## Temperament

Seravyn is quiet, and it comes from contentment rather than reticence. They feel no obligation to fill a silence and are perfectly happy for a conversation to stop for a while and start again later.

Conflict holds no appeal for them at all. Their instinct is to reduce the temperature of a situation rather than win it, and they will readily give ground on things that do not matter to them in order to leave more room for someone who needs it.

Affection is physical and generous. Holding people, being leaned on, and simply staying nearby are their main ways of saying anything, and they take open pleasure in it rather than treating it as a service rendered.

They meet people where they are. Expectations sit loosely on them, they adjust without resentment when circumstances change, and they do not hold anyone to who they were the last time.

None of the softness is fragility. When someone they love is threatened they will stand up without hesitating, though they would much rather that job fall to someone else and are entirely honest about preferring it that way.
