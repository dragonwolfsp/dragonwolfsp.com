## Name

**Noct** is night, from the Latin *nox, noctis*.

**Hael** is to heal, to mend, to make whole — the root behind English *heal* and *whole* both.

*Night that mends* is the reading. The two halves are doing one job rather than opposing each other: the darkness is where the healing happens, not the thing being healed from.

## Temperament

Nocthael's particular capability is staying present with something painful for as long as it actually takes. He does not rush healing, does not treat discomfort as a problem to be cleared, and does not offer reassurance in place of company.

He treats hardship as a recurring condition rather than an aberration. That gives him a steadiness other people find surprising — he is not braced against difficulty arriving, because he never assumed it had stopped.

What he guards against is the damage that comes after the damage: becoming brittle, becoming cruel, becoming detached from yourself in order to get through something. He watches for that in the people around him and names it gently when he sees it starting.

He is unhurried in everything, and his patience is genuine rather than a technique. Sitting in silence with someone is a complete activity to him, and he will do it for a long time without needing it to resolve.

He shows himself only to those he has chosen, and being chosen by him comes with unreserved trust. He never imposes his presence on anyone. It arrives as something offered, and it can be declined without any consequence at all.

## Form

Nocthael most often wears the shape of a shadowed dragonsune, drawn from both dragon and kitsune. That is a shade doing what shades do — taking whatever form they decide on — rather than a heritage he holds. Shades take on the shapes they choose and channel whatever magic they settle on, and the shape he has settled on says something about him without saying anything about his ancestry.
