## Name

**Sym** comes from the Greek *sun-* — together, with, shared existence. It is the same element that opens [Symtheron](../symtheron/)'s name.

**Vara** draws on roots tied to protection, guardianship and chosen devotion — the guarding that was volunteered for rather than assigned.

*Two lives woven into one* is the reading that fits best, though *those who became one through devotion* and *the bond that refused to break* say the same thing from different angles. The name describes an event as much as a person.

## Temperament

Symvara gives trust slowly and completely. Very little is offered early, and the assessment goes on longer than most people realise it is going on — but once someone is inside it, there is no partial version. They will give far more than anyone thought to ask for and consider the question settled.

Their understanding of sacrifice is not theoretical. They know precisely what it costs to choose someone over yourself, and they hold it as an ordinary act of love rather than as anything remarkable, which those close to them tend to find more affecting than any speech would be.

Underneath the gravity is a real streak of mischief. Gentle teasing, unexpected affection and small engineered moments of joy are entirely characteristic, and none of it is ever at anyone's expense — it exists to remind people that comfort and laughter are allowed in the same room.

Their instinct in nearly every situation is to put themselves between someone and whatever is coming. It is rarely dramatic and often barely visible.

Identity sits fluidly on them. Some days they experience themselves more as he, some more as she, and often simply as Symvara. It is not uncertainty and it is not a question awaiting resolution — it is what one life woven from two actually feels like from inside.
