## Name

**Renu** comes from the Sanskrit *reṇu*, dust: a mote, a single grain of it.

**Vira** comes from the Sanskrit *vīrā*, a heroine, a woman warrior.

*The warrior risen from dust* is the plain reading. It holds the turn of her inscription, from dust to life, along with the trade she chose for that life.

## Temperament

Renuvira is a storyteller, with a story for nearly everything and a gift for telling it well, though she is just as glad to sit and hear someone else's.

She is warm and open, the kind of person who makes others welcome without ever seeming to try.

Her loyalty runs fierce and deep. Fighting is her trade, and above all she uses it to protect her crew and the people close to her.

She is quietly proud, of where she has been and of what she has made of herself, and she lets that pride show in how she carries herself rather than in anything she says.
