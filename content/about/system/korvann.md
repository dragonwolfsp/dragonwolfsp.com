## Name

**Kor** draws on a family of roots clustered around the heart and the core — the innermost part of a thing, what remains when everything outward has been stripped away. Latin *cor* sits in that family, and so does the sense of "core" in English.

**Vann** draws on roots tied to oaths, bonds and loyalty that was chosen rather than inherited. The emphasis falls on the choosing: a bond entered into deliberately, by someone who could have declined.

*Keeper of the bond* is the plainest reading. Both halves point at the same thing from inside and outside — what he is at the centre, and what he has undertaken.

## Temperament

Korvann is quiet, and it is not reticence. He listens all the way to the end of what someone is saying, and when he does speak it is short, direct and free of hedging. Small talk is not something he manufactures.

Loyalty is the organising fact about him, and he understands it as something given rather than owed. He picked the people he stands with, and that is exactly why he does not treat it as negotiable. Betrayal is the thing that genuinely angers him — including the kind where somebody abandons themselves.

He does not perform strength and has no interest in demonstrating it. He never raises his voice to make a point, never postures, and would rather nothing happened at all than get an opportunity to act.

Secrets are safe with him to an unusual degree. People tell him things, partly because he does not react much and partly because he has never once passed anything along.

His compassion runs specifically toward people who have been discarded, and it shows up as practical help rather than sympathy — turning up, carrying something, standing nearby for as long as it takes.
