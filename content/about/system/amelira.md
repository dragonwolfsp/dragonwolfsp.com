## Name

**Amelira** takes its first element from the Latin *amare*, to love. The verb is the ordinary, everyday one rather than anything elevated — it covers affection between friends as readily as anything grander, which is the sense meant here.

**Lira** comes from *lyra*, the lyre. The instrument carries associations of music, harmony and expression given outward for others to hear.

The two together read as *song of love*, or affection that becomes harmony when it is shared. She shares the *lira* element with [Nolira](../nolira/), where it does slightly different work — shared voices in Nolira's name, expressed joy in hers.

## Temperament

Amelira is delighted by other people, more or less constantly, and makes very little effort to disguise it. Her pleasure at someone simply being present is unguarded to the point of being disarming.

She is playful without being chaotic. There is real care underneath the bounding and the teasing — she notices who has gone quiet, who is on the edge of a group, who is having a worse day than they are admitting to, and she moves toward them cheerfully enough that it never feels like being singled out.

Physical affection is her first language. She reaches, leans, drapes and wraps, usually before deciding to, and she is genuinely happy to be told no — it does not register as rejection and she does not go cold about it.

What she is actually good at is making a group cohere. Conversations run easier around her, and people who did not know each other an hour ago end up comfortable, largely because she is visibly enjoying all of them at once and it turns out to be contagious.

Sitting still is not her strength, and long stretches of solitude leave her flat in a way that is obvious to anyone who knows her.
