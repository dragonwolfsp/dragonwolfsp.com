## Name

**Ael** carries enduring flame — fire shaped by intent rather than fire as an event. The lasting kind, tended rather than kindled.

**Zephyra** is the feminine of *Zephyrus*, the gentle west wind of Greek tradition. Notably the mild wind, the one associated with spring rather than with storms.

*Flame carried on a steady wind* is the reading. The pairing is deliberate: fire needs air, and the air in her name is the patient sort, which is why the fire in it burns rather than blows out.

## Temperament

Aelzephyra is the calm that holds when a situation has stopped being calm. When things become chaotic she slows them down — not by taking charge, but by refusing to move at the speed the panic is setting, which turns out to be enough on its own more often than not.

She speaks only when she believes something needs saying, and when she does it is measured and deliberate. Silence from her is not withdrawal, and people who know her have stopped reading it that way.

Most of the comfort she offers arrives through presence rather than words. She stays, she does not fill the space, and she does not require anyone to explain themselves in order to be worth staying for.

She is patient in a way that occasionally unsettles people, because it is genuine rather than practised. She has endured long cycles and does not treat any particular crisis as decisive, which is steadying to be near and can be maddening to argue with.

Nothing about that is passivity. Her fire is entirely intact underneath, held rather than absent, and when a situation genuinely calls for it what has been patient turns out to be capable of being absolute.
