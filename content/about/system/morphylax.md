## Name

**Morph** comes from the Greek μορφή *morphē*, shape, form.

**Phylax** comes from the Greek φύλαξ *phylax*, guardian, keeper, watcher.

*Guardian of forms* is the reading. It holds both halves of him: the many shapes he carries, and the reason he carries them.

## Temperament

Morphylax is quiet, but open. He is reserved, and he isn't shy. Ask him something and he will talk with you openly and at length.

He cares deeply for the natural world around him, and he holds that his gifts exist to preserve and protect, both the world and everyone who lives in it.

His gender moves with his form. In his own body he is he. In a morphoderm his sense of himself leans with the body he is wearing, toward she as the otter or the tiefling, toward he as the lion or the wolf, and he prefers the pronouns that go with that form. Any of the three is welcome all the same, and he takes no offence at whichever one is used.

Sometimes he wants to lean another way for a while, and then he takes up a form that gives him that feeling and stays in it for as long as he wants. His gender is something he can step into, the way he steps into a skin.
