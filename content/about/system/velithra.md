## Name

**Vel** carries the veil: subtlety, and thought that is held rather than displayed.

**Lithra** draws on *lithe* and on *lithos* — structure, stability, the underlying frame of a thing. The same root sits in her lineage name, *Tokrous Lithos*, though there it describes build rather than mind.

*She who moves with quiet intellect* is the reading, or *veiled thinker of calm precision*. Softness over structure, in the name as in her.

## Temperament

Velithra wants to understand before she judges, and she is willing to spend a considerable amount of time on the first in order to postpone the second. It is not indecision. She simply holds that a conclusion reached too early is usually the wrong one and always a smaller one.

She is drawn to the questions underneath the questions — philosophy, study, and why a thing is the way it is rather than merely how it works. Ideas that reveal a hidden connection give her genuine, visible pleasure.

She has a quiet appetite for clever humour and subtle wit, and the joy she takes in it is mostly the joy of being understood without everything having to be said.

Her patience should not be read as passivity. She is soft-spoken and kind and by no means fragile, and where defending herself or someone she loves is called for she does not hesitate. Her compassion is tempered by judgement rather than substituting for it.

She is a calming influence without trying to be one, largely because she examines a situation from several angles as a matter of course, and being near that makes it easier for other people to step back from their own feelings and look at the thing itself.

She listens attentively and values understanding over assumption. Her care shows up as conversation, quiet support and a real interest in seeing people grow rather than as anything demonstrative.
