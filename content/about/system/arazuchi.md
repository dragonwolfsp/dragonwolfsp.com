## Name

**Ara** comes from the Japanese 荒, *ara*, wild or rough. It is also the start of *arashi*, 嵐, storm.

**Zuchi** comes from the Japanese 土, *tsuchi*, earth or soil. After another word it softens to *zuchi*, the same sound change that turns *tsuki* into *zuki* in *mikazuki*, the crescent moon.

*Wild earth* is the plain reading, or *earth with a storm in it*: the ground the storm walks on. It holds both of the elements Arazuchi is made of.

## Temperament

Arazuchi is warm and open with strangers, and welcomes them before they have had to ask.

She is protective and caring both, watching over the people near her and looking after them.

He values quiet, deep conversation and small, close-knit groups, and he holds trust and loyalty very highly, most of all within those groups.

Anyone who proves unkind, or a threat, loses that warmth, and finds Arazuchi uninterested in them or openly hostile. Unnecessary cruelty towards someone who has not deserved it turns them coldest of all.

Arazuchi's gender is fluid. He, she and they are all theirs, and this page moves between them.
