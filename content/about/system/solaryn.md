## Name

**Sol** is the sun, plainly and directly.

**Aryn** carries the sense of a guardian spirit — a protector attached to something in particular rather than protection as an abstract virtue.

*Guardian of the sun*, or nearer the intent, *the one the sunlight watches through*. Both halves point at the same job, and the name commits to it without qualification.

## Temperament

Solaryn's defining quality is that his loyalty was decided rather than inherited. He chose the people he stands for, and he treats the choosing as the reason it holds rather than as a formality that preceded it.

He is vigilant more or less continuously, and it costs him less than it would cost most people. Watching is restful to him. He notices what is off in a room long before it develops, and he does not need to say so in order to have already accounted for it.

Conflict is not something he goes looking for, and he does not retreat from it either. What he is good at is holding — maintaining a position under sustained pressure, staying steady when the situation has gone on far longer than anyone expected, and not degrading as it does.

His attention carries no judgement, which is unusual in someone who watches that closely. He is working out what needs defending and what needs challenging, and people generally feel examined without feeling assessed.

He translates care into action almost automatically. Concern that stays a feeling is not, to his mind, concern at all.

## The sun-tie

Every lion carries a tie to the sun. Solaryn is [sun-kissed](../species/breeds/sun-kissed/) and holds the deepest form of that connection the species reaches — it is why the gold in his coat and mane behaves the way it does, and why he runs larger than other lions.

The breed describes what he is connected to and nothing else. What he does with it is his.
