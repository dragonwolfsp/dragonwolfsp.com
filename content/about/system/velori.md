## Name

**Vel** carries subtle warmth — quiet presence, and emotion held rather than displayed.

**Ori** carries the heart: connection, affection, companionship.

*The quiet heart that grows into fierce devotion* is the sense of it. The name describes a process rather than a state, which is the right shape for someone whose warmth arrives on a delay and then does not go anywhere.

## Temperament

Velori is shy with people he does not know well, and it reads as reserve rather than nerves. He observes first, becomes comfortable in his own time, and only then lets the rest of himself show. Those who meet him once tend to come away with an incomplete picture.

What is underneath is considerably warmer than the surface suggests. His affection is deep and his loyalty is fierce, and both arrive gradually rather than all at once — his passion grows the closer someone gets rather than announcing itself early.

He believes strength is mostly a matter of people standing together, and he acts on it. He seeks harmony in a group, contributes toward whatever everyone else is working on rather than toward anything of his own, and takes real and visible pleasure in other people succeeding.

He is sensitive to how others are feeling, notices it quickly, and worries about it more than he probably should. His kindness shows up as encouragement, company and small unremarkable acts rather than as anything grand.

Gentle is not the same as weak in him. When someone he loves is threatened the warmth converts directly into determined protectiveness, and he defends his chosen family with a resolve that surprises everyone who had only seen the quiet version.
