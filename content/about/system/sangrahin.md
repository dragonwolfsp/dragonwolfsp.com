## Name

**Saṃgraha** is Sanskrit for a gathering-together — a collection, an assemblage, things brought into one place. The same word also means comprehension: grasping something, taking it in with the mind. Sanskrit treats collecting and understanding as one act, and does not distinguish between them.

**-in** is a Sanskrit agentive suffix: one who does this, one who has this.

*One who gathers* and *one who grasps* are the same reading. They have taken five lives permanently into one body, and what they do with every hour they are not doing that is try to understand something. The word was already there.

## Temperament

Very little rattles Sangrahin. They are entirely capable of excitement and it surfaces as interest — a thing catches, the attention sharpens, and the energy stays exactly where it was. The calm is their own. Of everything they carry that came from somewhere else, this is not part of it.

Underneath the calm is an appetite that does not close. They want to know things, they want to know why things are the way they are, and they will follow a question a long way past the point where it stopped being useful to anyone.

This is why they prefer three people to eight. Small groups and single conversations are where a subject can actually be opened, and a crowd never gets past the surface of anything. They ask a great deal, they love philosophy, and a question put to them comes back answered in full rather than acknowledged.

What earns their attention is someone who genuinely cares what is being said and genuinely wants to know things. Someone who does neither loses them quickly and without any scene being made about it.

What loses them entirely is carelessness about other people. Anyone indifferent to the effect they are having, or to what the people around them feel, gets coldness first — and coldness is where it stops only if it stops there. They care about how people feel with an intensity that sits oddly against the rest of their temperament, and cruelty on that front is the one thing capable of genuinely enraging them.

They have used the absorption more than once, and every time under one of two circumstances: it was offered to them as a gift, or the creature had to be stopped. They would do it again in either case and they carry no guilt about any of it. They would never use it outside those circumstances, and they hold anyone who would abuse it in open contempt.

They took memories from some of the creatures, and only ever the memories they had been told they could take. What arrived with the bodies arrived complete — the instincts belonging to five other lives are as much theirs now as they ever were their originals', and none of them runs ahead of them or acts on its own.

Their gender moves, and it moves unpredictably. Some stretches are he, some are she, some are they, and there is no schedule to it and no cause to look for. The body stays exactly as it is throughout. There is nothing in it waiting to be resolved and nothing in it that has yet to settle; this is simply how they run.
