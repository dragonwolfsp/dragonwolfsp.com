## Name

**Astr** comes from *astra* and *astron* — the stars, in both Latin and Greek, and the root behind *astral* and *astronomy*.

**Rhea** is the Titaness of Greek myth, associated with nurture, with flow, and with protection given rather than owed.

Together the name reads as *she who nurtures within the stars* — care carried across distance, in a place with a great deal of distance in it.

## The star markings

Astrrhea carries the geometric star markings woven through her coat, and among her people those are not decorative. Star markings indicate royal or warrior standing, usually both together, and the power producing them is granted in a ceremony held beneath the stars rather than inherited from nowhere. Once given, it passes to the holder's offspring, and it is taken back only if the population as a whole decides the holder has become unworthy of it.

Most vulperin carry no marking at all. Hers place her within a lineage that is expected to protect, and she has never treated that as a burden imposed on her.

## Temperament

Astrrhea gives affection openly and first. Where others wait to be sure of their welcome, she assumes it, and she is right often enough that the assumption has become a kind of gift she hands to people before they have asked for anything.

Her protectiveness is the nurturing kind rather than the defensive kind. Standing between someone and harm is part of it, but most of what she actually does is quieter — making sure the people within her reach know they are noticed, wanted and safe, and repeating it in small ways until they believe it.

She is genuinely vigilant underneath. She has been somewhere dangerous, moves calmly inside danger as a result, and is difficult to startle. That steadiness is what makes the warmth land rather than something separate from it.

Given the choice she takes connection over conflict every time, and healing over holding a line. She is entirely capable of the other thing and simply prefers not to reach for it.

She tends to arrive without announcement, stay longer than expected, and leave only once whatever brought her there has actually finished.
