## Name

**Ima** comes from the Latin *imus* — innermost, deepest, the lowest reach of a thing. *Ima* is its feminine form. It names the part of something you arrive at last, and only if you keep going.

**Thea** comes from the Greek θέα — a sight, a viewing, a beholding. It shares its root with *theasthai*, to behold and to contemplate, and it carries rather more weight than the ordinary verb for seeing does.

Together the name reads as *she who beholds what lies deepest*. Both halves point the same direction: past the surface, toward whatever is actually there. For someone who can wear any surface she likes, and who has no patience at all for people who read one and stop, it is the plainest description of her available.

## Temperament

Imathea likes people, and she likes her own company, and she has worked out the ratio well enough that neither one crowds the other. She will spend an evening in the middle of everything and take the next day entirely to herself, and she comes back from both restored.

Judging someone by what they look like is the fastest way to lose her good opinion. She has spent a long life wearing shapes that were never hers to begin with, and she knows exactly how little a surface is worth as evidence — which makes her patient with people who arrive looking like trouble, and very hard indeed on anyone who decides someone is worthless on sight.

She puts herself between bullies and whoever they have picked out, and she does it without much visible drama. Someone throwing their weight around will find the room has quietly rearranged itself against them, and that Imathea is somewhere in it being perfectly pleasant.

She teases relentlessly and takes it just as well. Praise lands on her properly and she lets it, and being teased by someone who loves her is one of her genuine pleasures.

The mystery is real and largely unintentional. She drops things into conversation that turn out, some hours later, to have been considerably larger than they sounded, and she is generally surprised that anyone was surprised.
