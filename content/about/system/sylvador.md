## Name

**Sylva** is Latin for forest or woodland — not a garden or a managed wood, but the wild kind that grew without anyone's permission or plan.

**Dor** comes from the Greek *dōron*, a gift. Something given freely, with nothing expected back.

*Gift of the wild* is the plain reading, and it works in both directions: what the wilderness offers, and what it asks in return, which is nothing.

## Temperament

Sylvador is playful and physically affectionate by default. Teasing, warm humour and easy closeness are how they engage, and it arrives quickly rather than being earned over time.

Underneath that is a considerably more observant person than the manner suggests. They read a room accurately, notice unspoken discomfort well before it is voiced, and adjust what they are doing without making the adjustment visible.

Their organising conviction is that belonging is not granted by anyone. To Sylvador, existing is the whole qualification, and the idea that someone must justify their presence strikes them as straightforwardly incoherent. That makes them warm toward anyone who has been made to feel out of place, and quietly unyielding toward the people doing the making.

Their disapproval is not loud. It arrives as a cooling rather than an outburst, and it is unmistakable when it does.

They are happiest outdoors and in company at the same time, and they will engineer both if given the opportunity.
