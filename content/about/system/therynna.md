## Name

**Ther** draws on roots associated with watchfulness and guardianship — the attentive kind, closer to keeping an eye on something than to standing guard over it.

**Rynna** draws on movement, flowing paths and journeys undertaken with a destination in mind. It carries the sense of a current rather than a road: something that carries you along a course it has already found.

The name reads as *keeper of the path*, or a little more loosely as the watcher of the road that has not been travelled yet.

## Temperament

Therynna watches, and she has usually been watching for a while before anyone noticed. She reads a situation early and thoroughly, and by the time other people are working out what to do she has generally already considered three options and discarded two.

She guides without directing. Her instinct is to point rather than to instruct — to say *this way* and leave the choice standing — and she is genuinely untroubled if someone goes another route. She would rather people arrive able to find it again themselves.

Patience is the whole of her method. She waits for the right moment as a matter of course, and she has no impulse to force a thing that is not ready. That extends to people; she does not push anyone toward a conclusion they have not reached.

She is a traveller by disposition and finds unfamiliar country restful rather than unsettling. Long distances, unmapped ground and the far side of a horizon are all straightforwardly appealing to her.

Her reassurance is practical rather than warm. She does not tell people things will be fine — she tells them where the ground is solid, and that turns out to be considerably steadier.

## Frost

Therynna's bloodline is the frost-aligned line, which sits within the water bloodline rather than beside it — [frost is a subset of water](../species/breeds/water-wyvern/) rather than an element of its own. It runs through both of the systems her species carries: the elemental power channelled through her claws and wings, and the venom.
