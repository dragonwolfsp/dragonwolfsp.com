## Name

**Leik** comes from the Old Norse *leikr*: play, sport, a game. The word covered a great deal of ground, from children's games to contests of strength to the quick back-and-forth of trading words, and it survives in the modern Scandinavian words for play.

**Eisa** is Old Norse for glowing embers, the live coals left when a fire has burned down and is still very much alight.

*Playing embers* is the direct reading, and it is close to literal for a hellhound whose fire moves along her coat. The better sense is the quiet coal that comes alive when something stirs it, and what stirs her, most often, is play.

## Temperament

Leikeisa is quiet, and her quiet runs deep enough that it reads as stoic. She watches, she listens, and she is content to say very little for long stretches. People who only meet her in passing tend to come away with that stillness and nothing else.

Underneath it lives a deeply playful spirit. She loves to play with her whole body, chasing, wrestling, pouncing and being pounced on, and she loves it just as much with words. Banter is a game to her like any other, and she is quick at it, dry and well aimed, and delighted when someone answers in kind.

She loves movement for its own sake. Running, roaming, going out and doing something with the people she loves are among her favourite things in the world, and an invitation to come along is one of the warmest things she offers.

Her protectiveness is steady and constant. She keeps track of her people, and she places herself between them and trouble as a matter of course. Unjust harm is the thing that angers her most. When someone sets out to hurt another who has done nothing to deserve it, the fire in her eyes turns coal red, and all of that quiet becomes very focused.

Once someone is close to her, the warmth under the stillness is constant, and the play is never far behind it.
