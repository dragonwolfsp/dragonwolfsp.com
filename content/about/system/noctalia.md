## Name

**Noct** comes from the Latin *nox*, *noctis* — night. Not night as absence or as something to be waited out, but night as its own condition, with its own quiet.

**Alia** comes from *alius* — other, another, a different one. It is the ordinary Latin word for someone outside the group being spoken about, and it carries no judgement in itself; that gets added by whoever uses it.

The two together read as *she who welcomes the other beneath the night*. The second element is the load-bearing one — the name is built around the person nobody has made room for, and the night is the hour in which room finally exists.

## Temperament

Noctalia is soft-spoken and genuinely unbothered by being overlooked. She has no appetite for attention and does not compete for airtime, which means she is often the last person in a room to say anything and frequently the one people end up talking to.

Her particular skill is making it safe to stop performing. She does not probe, does not offer solutions unasked, and does not treat someone's silence as a problem to be fixed. What she offers is company without conditions, and it works on people who have spent a long time managing how they come across.

She understands being out of place from the inside rather than by sympathy, and it has made her patient instead of bitter. She is unshockable in a useful way — very little that anyone tells her produces a reaction she has to hide.

Quiet suits her and she seeks it out. Late hours, dark water, the part of an evening after most people have gone. She is at her most present there, and she is not lonely in it.

None of that makes her passive. She is difficult to move once she has settled on something, and her gentleness has never been the same thing as agreement.
