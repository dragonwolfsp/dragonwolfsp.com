## Name

**Ae** derives from *aequus* — level, even, fair. The root behind *equity* and *equilibrium*, and the sense is balance as a measured state rather than as a compromise.

**Vira** carries strength and force.

*Balanced strength* is the reading, and the two halves modify each other in both directions: force held level, and balance that has power behind it rather than merely wishing for it.

## Temperament

Aevira watches over time rather than reacting in the moment. A single incident tells her very little; what she is looking for is the pattern, and she will let a great deal pass without comment while she establishes whether one exists.

That makes her look passive to people who have not worked her out. She is not. She is deciding, and the interval between noticing and acting is the whole of her method rather than hesitation within it.

When she does act it is precise, proportionate and final. She does not escalate for effect, does not issue warnings she has not thought through, and does not repeat herself. Her authority carries no aggression in it at all, which is part of why it lands.

What she will not tolerate is a boundary violated repeatedly. Once has explanations available; a pattern does not, and she treats the difference as the entire question.

She is the one who works out when enough is enough — for others as much as for herself. She has a clear view that mercy extended past its limit becomes self-erasure, and that restraint held too long stops being restraint and becomes inaction.
