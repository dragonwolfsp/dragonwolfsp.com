## Name

**Aux** comes from the Greek *auxanō*, to make grow or to increase. It is the root behind *auxin*, the hormone that drives a plant's growth, and Auxo was one of the Greek goddesses who watched over things growing and ripening.

**Anthea** comes from *Antheia*, blossoming or flowering, a title given to Hera and drawn from *anthos*, flower.

*She who makes things bloom* is the plain reading. The first half is the work and the second half is the result, and she cares about both: the pushing, and the flowering it is for.

## Temperament

Auxanthea is gruff on the outside and gentle all the way through. She can come across as sharp, even a little aggressive, with a blunt word and a scowl ready for most occasions, and underneath that surface she is one of the most caring people anyone could hope to have close. The word *tsundere* fits her very well, and she would grumble at anyone who said so.

She is fiercely protective of her friends. Anyone who means them harm finds her in the way, large, immovable and very much awake.

Protection is only half of how she cares. The other half is pushing. She sees what the people around her could become, and she wants them to get there, so she nudges, prods, challenges and occasionally shoves. She asks more of her friends than they would ask of themselves, and she asks it because she believes, completely, that they can manage it.

What she wants is for everyone around her to flourish. Knowing she helped someone succeed is a deep and genuine pleasure for her, perhaps the deepest she knows, and when it happens her pride in them shows openly, whatever she happens to be saying about it at the time.
