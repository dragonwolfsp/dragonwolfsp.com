## Overview

A void is nothingness made living. To exist is to be something, and a void is the absence of everything, so every void in this reality is holding itself back: restricting how much nothing it is, until what remains is enough of a something to exist here at all.

What a void becomes in doing so is another creature. Voids have no native form of their own, and they manifest by becoming one.

Where voids come from is not wholly known. What is known is that some part of the void decides to bind itself to a reality, and that a void begins there.

Every void's soul is drawn from the aether, as every soul is. The void they are is carried through the whole of it, and it never leaves them completely.

## The becoming

Voids call the act of being born into this reality the becoming. It is the moment a void constrains itself into a form and takes on another creature as their own.

The form taken in the becoming may be a single species or a blend of several. Whatever it is, the void carries it in full. A void who becomes a luperin speaks mind to mind as every luperin does, and whatever a species fixes, the void who becomes one carries too, along with any magic the becoming grants.

The becoming can be undergone again. It is never instant: how long it takes depends on the void's power. It also has a cost. A void who becomes something new often loses parts of who they were as a person along the way.

## Physical traits

A void's body is the body of whatever they have become, and it follows that creature's build, senses and structure.

The void they carry shows through it somehow, in a way that runs outside the rules of this reality. Some voids seem to absorb all light. Some are lit when there is no light to fall on them. Others carry an effect all their own. How it shows is individual, and no two voids need to share it.

## Magic

Every void carries void-born magic. It is an [extra force](../../magic/) rather than a fundamental one, and it breaks the rules the rest of magic follows. That much is fixed by the species, and it sits alongside whatever magic they gain from the becoming.

Void-born magic is hard to define, because this reality forcibly restricts it. Most often it shows as travel through the void: a void can step into it and come out anywhere they have an awareness of, whether that awareness is visual, mental or magical. That includes other realities.

A void with significant power can change at least parts of what they are. It takes skill and a great deal of underlying strength.

The strongest voids can make alterations to reality itself, changing things as they please. Those changes are never large or sweeping, though where that line sits is unclear.

The void that voids are made of is unrelated to the void the [vulperin](../vulperin/) Void Blessed draw on. The two share a word and nothing else.

Beyond void-born magic and whatever the becoming grants, a void's magic is their own.

## Shared instincts

Voids share their nature as nothingness held in check, the lack of a native form, the void carried in the soul, the becoming, and void-born magic. That is the list.

They share no temperament. Who a void is belongs to that void alone, and is recorded on their own page.

## Where the name comes from

The word is English, from Latin by way of Old French, and means empty: a space with nothing in it. Ours are that emptiness given life.

People often describe voids as eldritch, meaning something beyond their understanding. It is a fair description of how a void can seem from the outside, and it is not a word the system uses for them.

## Field notes

The form a void has become, how the void shows through it, the reach of their void-born magic and any magic gained from the becoming all belong on each member's own page.
