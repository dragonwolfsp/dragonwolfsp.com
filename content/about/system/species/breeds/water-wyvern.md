> **Content note.** Describes the venom's pleasurable expression alongside its harmful one.

## What differs

Water wyverns carry the water bloodline through both the elemental power in their claws and wings and the venom.

Delivered to harm, the venom fills and drowns, settling as a heaviness in the chest. Delivered for pleasure, it produces a warmth closer to being submerged and carried.

## The frost-aligned

Frost is a subset of water rather than an element of its own, and frost-aligned wyverns sit within this bloodline rather than beside it.

Their venom behaves differently from the rest of the line. Delivered to harm it numbs and stiffens. Delivered for pleasure it produces a cool weightlessness — a numbness that reads as floating rather than as loss of feeling.

## Magic

The element is fixed by the bloodline. The venom is an extra force. Delivery by bite, tail or both is independent of the bloodline, and everything else belongs to the individual.
