## What differs

Jungle luperin are those born to the jungle region of the luperin homeworld, and they are built for moving without being heard.

They are possibly the smallest of the three lineages, favouring a lean build over a heavy one, with smaller footpaws than either the frost or desert lineages carry.

The pawpads are what genuinely sets them apart, and nothing else in the species has them: extraordinarily thick and squishy, they deaden footfall almost completely and protect the footpaws from injury on rough ground. A jungle luperin moving deliberately is very difficult to hear.

Coats run shorter and lighter than the frost lineages carry, with noticeably thinner underfur.
