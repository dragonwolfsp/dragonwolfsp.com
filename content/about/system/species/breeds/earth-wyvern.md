> **Content note.** Describes the venom's pleasurable expression alongside its harmful one.

## What differs

Earth wyverns carry the earth bloodline through both the elemental power in their claws and wings and the venom.

Delivered to harm, the venom stiffens the body toward petrification, with a crushing weight behind it. Delivered for pleasure, the same weight becomes something else entirely — a heavy, settled, grounded calm rather than a pressure.

Delivery by bite, tail or both does not follow from the bloodline; any wyvern of any element may carry any of the three.

## Magic

The element is fixed by the bloodline. The venom is an extra force. Everything else belongs to the individual.
