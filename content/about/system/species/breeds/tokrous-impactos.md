## What differs

*Tokrous Impactos* is the heavier tokren lineage. Where a *Lithos* carries moth-like fluff over finer plating, an *Impactos* carries considerably thicker chitin and no fluff whatsoever — the plating is the surface, visible and unsoftened.

They fly as readily as any other tokren. Their wings simply run broader and slightly thicker to carry the extra mass.

Height and the mantis-like grace are unchanged; those belong to the species rather than to either lineage, and an *Impactos* moves with exactly the same fluidity.

## Magic

Nothing beyond the species. The dance generates the same magic here as anywhere else, and everything else an *Impactos* is tied to is their own.
