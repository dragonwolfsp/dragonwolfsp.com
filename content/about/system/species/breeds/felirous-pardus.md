## What differs

*Felirous Pardus* is the feliry lineage that most resembles the Earth leopard. The lineage sets a build register: leaner and more compact than *Felirous Leo*, carrying poised strength rather than mass.

Nothing else follows from it. Coat length, colouring, integration, whether they have merged with an artificial intelligence, and temperament are all the individual's own.

## Magic

Nothing is fixed at the lineage level, and nothing is fixed at the species level either. Any magical tie a *Felirous Pardus* carries is their own.
