## What differs

Lunari are the western form: four legs, two wings, and heavy scaling across the body.

It is the body plan most people picture when they hear the word dragon, and the divergence from a Solari is structural — four limbs plus a separate wing pair, against the Solari's serpentine length.

The folklore behind the form is almost entirely antagonistic: the hoard in the barrow, the beast to be killed, Fáfnir and the wyrm of *Beowulf* and the dragon of the St George legend. None of that carries over. We took the shape and left the story where it was.

## Magic

The element is a fundamental affinity following bloodline rather than body plan — a Lunari may carry any of them — and it is channelled through the breath primarily and through the tail for more complex work, exactly as it is for any dragon.

Whatever else a Lunari is tied to is their own.
