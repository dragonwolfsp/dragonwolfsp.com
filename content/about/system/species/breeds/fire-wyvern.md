> **Content note.** Describes the venom's pleasurable expression alongside its harmful one.

## What differs

Fire wyverns carry the fire bloodline, and it runs through both of the species' systems at once — the elemental power channelled through claws and wings, and the venom.

Delivered to harm, the venom burns and cauterises as it spreads. Delivered for pleasure, the same underlying venom produces a spreading heat and a deep flush instead. Which one arrives is the wyvern's choice, made in the moment.

Whether a fire wyvern delivers through the bite, the tail or both does not follow from the bloodline. Any wyvern of any element may have any of the three, though tail-only is rare.

## Magic

The element is fixed by the bloodline. The venom is an extra force. Everything else belongs to the individual.
