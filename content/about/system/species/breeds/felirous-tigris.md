## What differs

*Felirous Tigris* is the feliry lineage that most resembles the Earth tiger, and carries the largest build of the five — heavily muscled, particularly through the forequarters, without losing the flexibility every feliry has.

The lineage determines the frame and stops there. Coat, colouring, integration, whether they have merged with an artificial intelligence, and temperament are all the individual's own.

## Magic

Nothing is fixed at the lineage level, and nothing is fixed at the species level either. Any magical tie a *Felirous Tigris* carries is their own.
