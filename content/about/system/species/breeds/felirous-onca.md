## What differs

*Felirous Onca* is the feliry lineage that most resembles the Earth jaguar: stocky, powerfully built, and noticeably broad through the head and jaw compared with the leaner lineages.

That build register is the whole of what the lineage sets. Coat, colouring, integration, whether they have merged with an artificial intelligence, and temperament are all individual.

## Magic

Nothing is fixed at the lineage level, and nothing is fixed at the species level either. Any magical tie a *Felirous Onca* carries is their own.
