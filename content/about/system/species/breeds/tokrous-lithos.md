## What differs

*Tokrous Lithos* is the lighter of the two tokren lineages: slender, delicate through the limbs, and carrying the fine moth-like fluff most people picture when they picture a tokren. The fluff is heaviest across chest and shoulders, thinning along the torso and becoming sparse over the limbs, where the smooth contours of the chitin show through.

The plating beneath is thinner than an *Impactos* carries, which is what the build costs. It is still real protection, and the lineage is not fragile — only lighter.

Wings run finer and lighter to match.

## Magic

Nothing beyond the species. The dance generates the same magic here as anywhere else, and everything else a *Lithos* is tied to is their own.
