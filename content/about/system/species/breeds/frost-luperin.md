## What differs

Frost luperin are those born to the frost region of the luperin homeworld, and the cold has shaped them more than it has shaped either of the other lineages.

The coat is the thickest and densest in the species, layered heavily enough to keep the cold out entirely rather than merely blunt it.

The footpaws are the other obvious difference: larger and considerably wider than a jungle or desert luperin carries, spreading weight so a frost luperin can move over deep snow instead of through it.

Build runs to the larger end of the species range, which the coat and the paws both suit.

## Magic

Nothing beyond the species trait. The head striping works identically here, on the same natural frequency and with the same pack-dependent range.
