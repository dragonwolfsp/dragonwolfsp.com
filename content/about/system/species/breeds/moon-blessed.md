## What differs

Every wolf carries some tie to the moon. The moon-blessed carry it at a strength no other wolf comes close to.

They are the one wolf breed that is not simply a natural subspecies. The rest of the breed division follows ordinary wolf biology — timber, arctic and the others — where this one follows the magic instead, and a moon-blessed wolf may come from any of those lines.

They tend to be larger and stronger than other wolves, and the difference is noticeable rather than marginal.

They are rare.

## Magic

The moon-tie is exceptionally powerful in a moon-blessed wolf, and it lets them do things no other wolf can — not simply more of what other wolves manage, but work that is closed to the rest of the species outright.

Everything beyond the moon-tie is individual, exactly as it is for any other wolf. Being moon-blessed says what someone is capable of. It says nothing at all about what they do with it, or about who they are.
