## What differs

Every lion carries a tie to the sun. The sun-kissed carry the greatest connection to it in the species.

They are the one lion breed that is not simply a natural subspecies. The rest of the breed division follows ordinary lion biology, where this one follows the magic instead, and a sun-kissed lion may come from any of those lines.

They typically run larger than other lions.

## Magic

The sun-tie is at its deepest in a sun-kissed lion, and reaches well past what other lions can do with theirs.

Everything beyond it is individual, exactly as it is for any other lion. Being sun-kissed describes what someone is connected to. It says nothing about what they do with it, or about who they are.
