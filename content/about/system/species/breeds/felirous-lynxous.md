## What differs

*Felirous Lynxous* is the feliry lineage that most resembles the Earth lynx. The lineage sets a build register and nothing else: the most compact frame of the five, short-coupled through the body, with legs long relative to it.

Coat, colouring, whether they carry synthetic integration at all, whether they have merged with an artificial intelligence, and what kind of person they are, are all individual.

## Magic

Nothing is fixed at the lineage level, and nothing is fixed at the species level either. Any magical tie a *Felirous Lynxous* carries is their own.
