## What differs

*Felirous Leo* is the feliry lineage that most resembles the Earth lion, and takes its name accordingly. The lineage sets a build register rather than anything else: deep, weighty force through the chest and shoulders, heavier than the species' midpoint without losing the flexibility every feliry has.

That is the whole of what the lineage determines. Coat, colouring, whether a *Felirous Leo* carries synthetic integration at all, whether they have merged with an artificial intelligence, and what kind of person they are, are all individual.

## Magic

Nothing is fixed at the lineage level, and nothing is fixed at the species level either. Any magical tie a *Felirous Leo* carries is their own.
