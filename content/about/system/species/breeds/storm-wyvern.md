> **Content note.** Describes the venom's pleasurable expression alongside its harmful one.

## What differs

Storm wyverns carry the storm bloodline — wind and electricity together, as the element covers both — through the elemental power in their claws and wings and through the venom alike.

Delivered to harm, the venom causes convulsion. Delivered for pleasure, the same electrical character becomes tingling waves and shivers running through the body rather than seizure.

Delivery by bite, tail or both does not follow from the bloodline; any wyvern of any element may carry any of the three.

## Magic

The element is fixed by the bloodline. The venom is an extra force. Everything else belongs to the individual.
