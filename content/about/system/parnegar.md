## Name

**Par** comes from the Persian پر, *par*, a wing or a feather.

**Negar** comes from the Persian نگار, *negār*, a painted or inscribed design. In Persian poetry the same word also means a beloved.

*The inscribed wing* is the plain reading. It holds the runes written across their armour and the wings that unfold from it, and the second meaning of negār suits someone so open and gentle with the people around them.

## Temperament

Parnegar is playful and gentle most of the time, with a light, teasing humour that comes out once someone knows them.

She is open to new connections, curious about new people and glad to know them.

He is protective of what is his, and has no trouble standing firm, or fighting, when it is needed, which is much of why his armour is built the way it is.

They are a very steady presence, rarely unsettled by anything at all.

Parnegar is aporagender. She, he and they are all theirs, and this page moves between them as freely as they do.
