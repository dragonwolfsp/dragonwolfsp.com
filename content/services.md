Beyond the stories and signals of this domain, a number of small tools quietly run behind the scenes.

This page serves as a guide to those public services — utilities we operate for anyone who may find them useful. Some are simple instruments, others are gateways for communication or sharing, but each one is maintained here as part of the wider Dragonwolf network.

Think of these as well-lit paths through the digital wilderness. If you have wandered this far, you are welcome to make use of them.

---

## [EchoIP](//ifconfig.dragonwolfsp.com)

A simple service that tells you the public IP address your connection appears to come from.

---

## [ntfy](//ntfy.dragonwolfsp.com)

A push notification service that allows applications and scripts to send messages through publish/subscribe topics.

---

## [send](//send.dragonwolfsp.com)

A temporary file sharing service for uploading files and sharing them through short-lived links.
