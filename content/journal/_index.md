Life is made of more than milestones.

Beyond the grand tales lie the quieter moments that give them meaning. Welcome **Along the Trail**, where each step leaves its own impression. Here we gather reflections, discoveries, aspirations, and the everyday experiences that shape our path, weaving them together into a chronicle of the journey as it is lived.

Some entries may be only a few paragraphs. Others may grow into longer reflections. Together, they form a record of the journey as it happens.

The trail is always changing, and so are we.
