## Voice Direction

Goreth should sound disciplined, guarded, and emotionally conflicted beneath a controlled exterior. His voice carries the weight of someone who has spent a long time compromising parts of himself in order to survive within a system he no longer fully believes in.

He is not openly cruel, but there is hardness in him shaped by duty, fear, and resignation. When speaking formally, he sounds measured and authoritative. In more personal moments, subtle exhaustion and frustration should bleed through the cracks in his composure.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "You think I enjoy this? You think any of us had a choice?"

> "The Coalition doesn’t care what we believe. Only whether we obey."

> "Do not mistake survival for loyalty."
