## Voice Direction

Mira should sound warm, perceptive, and emotionally present. Her voice carries natural openness, with an ease in expression that makes her feel approachable even in tense situations. She is attentive to people and surroundings, often responding in a way that acknowledges both spoken and unspoken emotion.


Despite her gentler presence, she is not fragile. Under pressure, her tone sharpens with quiet resolve rather than panic. She expresses concern directly and sincerely, sometimes with an almost disarming honesty that contrasts more guarded personalities around her.
