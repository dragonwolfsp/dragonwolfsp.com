## Voice Direction

Isaac should sound sharp, experienced, and slightly unhinged in a controlled sort of way. He carries the energy of someone who is constantly thinking several steps ahead, often speaking with dry humor or cynical observations even during dangerous situations.

Despite the rough edges, he is not bitter. There is still curiosity and genuine care beneath the sarcasm, especially around people he trusts. His voice should feel intelligent and fast-moving, with occasional intensity when discussing technology, experimentation, or systems he finds fascinating.

He has the demeanor of someone who has survived too much to romanticize the world, but not enough to stop trying to improve it anyway.

> "Do you have any idea how difficult it is to keep illegal biotech stable in field conditions?"

> "People keep calling my work dangerous. Funny thing is, they only say that right before asking me to save them with it."

> "The galaxy runs on broken systems and terrified people pretending they understand them."
