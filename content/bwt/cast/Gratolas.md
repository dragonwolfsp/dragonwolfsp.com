## Voice Direction

Gratolas should sound ancient, deliberate, and deeply controlled. His voice carries natural authority without effort or performance. Every word feels considered before it is spoken, as though it has already been weighed against centuries of experience.

He is not warm in a conventional sense, but he is not hostile either. His tone is steady, almost elemental—like something old enough to have stopped needing to prove itself. When he speaks about freedom or the world, there should be quiet conviction rather than passion.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Freedom is not given. It is returned."

> "I have watched kingdoms rise and fall. Power always believes it is eternal."

> "If the world is to change, it will not do so gently."
