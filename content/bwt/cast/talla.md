## Voice Direction

Talla should sound highly intelligent, calm under pressure, and slightly unpredictable in a way that reflects creative problem-solving. Her speech is precise and efficient, often cutting directly to conclusions rather than explanations. She processes problems quickly and tends to verbalize solutions as they form, sometimes mid-thought.

Despite her analytical mind, she is not emotionally detached. There is a controlled intensity to her focus, and a subtle streak of chaotic creativity that appears when conventional approaches fail. In stressful situations, she becomes even more functional, not less—calm, fast, and unsettlingly confident in unconventional outcomes.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "That won’t work. Give me ten seconds."

> "We don’t need a perfect solution. We need one that survives contact with reality."

> "If it shouldn’t work but does… that’s usually where I start."
