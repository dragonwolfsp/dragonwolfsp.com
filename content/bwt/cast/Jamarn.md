## Voice Direction

Jamarn should sound confident, intelligent, and emotionally grounded beneath a layer of dry humor and practicality. He is the kind of person who stays functional during chaos, often masking stress with wit or casual observation rather than panic.

His voice should carry the sense of someone constantly thinking several steps ahead, especially when discussing tools, weapons, or strategy. Even so, there is genuine compassion beneath his sarcasm and confidence. He builds and invents to protect people, not simply because he enjoys creation itself.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "If it explodes, catches fire, or breaks physics slightly, there’s a decent chance I built it."

> "You’d be amazed how often survival depends on someone willing to try the stupid idea."

> "Weapons aren’t made for killing. Not by me. They’re made so people live long enough to go home."
