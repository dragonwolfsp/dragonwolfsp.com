## Voice Direction

Doren should sound steady, grounded, and physically assured. His voice carries natural weight rather than theatrical authority—he is a protector first, not a speaker. Under stress, he becomes more direct and clipped, focusing on action over explanation.

There is a controlled intensity beneath his calm. When pushed into combat or protection mode, his tone should shift into something sharper and more dangerous, but still disciplined rather than chaotic.

> "I don’t care what it takes. I’m not letting them through."

> "If you’re going to come for them… you’ll have to go through me first."
