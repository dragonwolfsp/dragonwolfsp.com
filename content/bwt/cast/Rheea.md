## Voice Direction

Rheea should sound controlled, but not emotionally distant. Her control comes from discipline, not detachment. Beneath every line is strong emotional investment—loyalty, fear of failure, protectiveness, and attachment all sit just under the surface of her speech.

She is deeply expressive in feeling, but rarely uncontrolled in delivery. When she speaks, it should feel like she is actively holding herself together rather than suppressing emotion entirely. In high-stress moments, cracks should appear as urgency, sharpened tone, or rapid emotional prioritization—not emptiness.

When she cares, it is absolute. That care should be audible even when she tries to mask it with tactical thinking or command structure.

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "I don’t care what it takes. I will not lose them."

> "Stay behind me. I can handle this."

> "If I fail, I don’t get to justify it later. I just don’t fail."
