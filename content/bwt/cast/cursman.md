## Voice Direction

Cursman should sound direct, emotionally sincere, and quietly intense. Unlike more elusive or manipulative personalities, he speaks openly and with very little performative masking. His voice carries both warmth and exhaustion, shaped by someone who continues protecting others despite rejection, fear, or mistrust.

There is strength in his delivery, but also restraint. He should sound like someone constantly holding destructive power under control—not through fear of himself, but through determination to never become what others expect him to be.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "I’m not asking you to trust me. I’m asking you to let me help."

> "People see fire and assume destruction. They never notice what it protects."

> "If the world insists on calling me a monster, then fine. I’ll still save it."
