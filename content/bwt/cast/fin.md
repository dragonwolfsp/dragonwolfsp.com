## Voice Direction

Fin should sound emotionally open, energetic, and instinct-driven. He speaks quickly when excited or stressed, often reacting before fully thinking things through. There is a youthful edge to his voice, but also underlying emotional weight shaped by abandonment, grief, and survival.

He tends to assume danger first and trust second, especially when the people he cares about are threatened. Even so, his loyalty is immediate and fierce once someone earns it. Beneath the defensiveness and impulsiveness is someone deeply afraid of losing the few connections he has left.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "I told them we couldn’t win. Nobody listened."

> "You don’t leave people behind. Not if you can still reach them."

> "Maybe I expect the worst because I’ve seen what happens when nobody does."
