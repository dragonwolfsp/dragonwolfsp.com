## Voice Direction

Solare should sound elegant, controlled, and deliberately composed. Her speech is often precise and slightly theatrical, as though she is always aware of how she is being perceived. There is a natural charm to her voice, but it is not purely warm—it carries intention behind it.

When relaxed, she can sound almost playful or teasing. Under pressure, that composure tightens, revealing something sharper and more guarded beneath the surface. Even in vulnerability, she rarely sounds truly exposed—only carefully managed.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Careful. Not everything that smiles is harmless."

> "If you wish to understand me, you will have to stop assuming you already do."

> "Monsters survive by being underestimated."
