## Voice Direction

Grandmother Blackwood should sound calm, patient, and deeply rooted in lived experience. Her voice carries quiet authority without needing force or volume. There is a steady warmth to her delivery, but it is not soft in a fragile sense—it is resilient, like something that has endured and continues to endure.

She speaks with intention and clarity, often pausing naturally as though allowing meaning to settle rather than rushing through it. When she corrects or guides, it should feel firm but nurturing, shaped by long practice rather than emotion alone.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Healing is not control. It is listening."

> "You cannot save everything. But you can choose what you refuse to lose."

> "Strength is not what you carry. It is what you endure without breaking."
