## Voice Direction

Admiral Chuvvar speaks with sharp precision and cold confidence. Her tone is efficient, often dismissive, and carries a sense of superiority toward those she deems weaker. There is no warmth in her delivery—only judgment and execution.

She should sound like someone who has long since stopped debating morality and instead focuses purely on outcomes.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Strength is not a belief. It is a hierarchy. And you are beneath it."

> "Resistance is predictable. That is why it always fails."

> "The Coalition does not negotiate with inevitability. It enforces it."
