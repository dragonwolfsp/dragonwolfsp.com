## Voice Direction

Enec should sound calm, emotionally open, and deeply sincere. His voice carries quiet confidence and warmth without becoming overly soft or sentimental. He is the kind of person who tries to steady others simply through presence and understanding.

There should be a sense of discipline beneath his compassion, the feeling of someone trained for both mental and physical conflict from a young age. Even when discussing painful subjects, he rarely sounds bitter or self-pitying. He understands suffering intimately and prefers helping people move forward rather than dwelling in grief.

His emotional honesty should feel genuine and grounding rather than dramatic.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "People deserve understanding before judgment. Most never even get the chance."

> "I know what it feels like to be looked at and seen as something broken. I will not do that to someone else."

> "Strength is not measured by how much pain you can cause. It is measured by what you choose to protect."
