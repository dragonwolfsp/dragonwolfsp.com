## Voice Direction

Grant should sound grounded, practical, and quietly authoritative. His tone is not ceremonial or distant—he speaks like someone who has lived through survival rather than theory. There is a steady, no-nonsense quality to his delivery, with an underlying warmth that becomes more apparent in moments of instruction or memory.

He is not overly emotional in expression, but he is deeply caring. That care comes through in what he chooses to teach, protect, and remember, rather than in overt sentimentality. When serious, his voice should feel immovable—like experience turned into instinct.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "You don’t win out there by being strong. You win by being ready."

> "If you hesitate in the forest, you die. Simple as that."

> "Survival isn’t about control. It’s about understanding what you can’t control."
