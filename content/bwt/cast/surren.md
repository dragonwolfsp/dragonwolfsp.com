## Voice Direction

Surren should sound composed, steady, and physically assured. Her presence carries weight without effort, and her voice reflects disciplined control under pressure. She is not emotionally distant, but she is highly regulated—rarely wasting words and rarely showing vulnerability in an obvious way.

When speaking to others, she often comes across as direct and slightly intimidating, especially in tense situations. Beneath that exterior, there is a strong sense of responsibility and protective instinct that occasionally surfaces in brief, softened moments or quiet concern.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Form up. Stay sharp. We don’t get second chances out here."

> "If you hesitate, someone dies. Decide faster next time."

> "I didn’t come this far to fail now."
