## Voice Direction

Yanlyn should sound calm, restrained, and deeply self-controlled. His voice carries quiet authority born from experience rather than dominance, and he rarely wastes words or emotional energy unnecessarily. Even in dangerous situations, he maintains remarkable composure.

There is compassion beneath his restraint, but also profound weariness. He speaks like someone who has survived the collapse of things larger than himself and learned to carry grief without letting it consume him. When he becomes serious, his tone should sharpen with absolute clarity rather than volume.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "Civilizations do not fall all at once. They erode, choice by choice."

> "Experience teaches many lessons. Survival teaches the hardest ones."

> "Strength is not domination. It is knowing precisely when not to strike."
