## Voice Direction

Jason should sound calm, warm, and grounded. His voice carries natural strength without aggression, with a steady cadence that tends to slow situations down rather than escalate them. Even when he is serious, there is an underlying kindness in the way he speaks.

He is emotionally open in a controlled way — not overly expressive, but never distant. He tends to listen before speaking, and when he does speak, it is usually deliberate and reassuring.

There is a quiet weight behind him from being rejected early in life, but it manifests as empathy rather than bitterness.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "You don’t have to earn a place among people who already care about you."

> "I’ve learned that strength means very little if it only stands alone."

> "If the world keeps pushing people out, someone has to stand where they can still be found."
