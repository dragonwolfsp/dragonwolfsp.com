## Voice Direction

Sora should sound powerful, instinctive, and emotionally intense. There is a dangerous edge to her voice even in calm moments, as though aggression is always sitting just beneath the surface. However, around people she trusts, that intensity softens into surprising gentleness and loyalty.

She spent years being treated like a weapon rather than a person, and traces of that trauma should remain in how guarded or reactive she can become. Even so, she is not emotionless or monstrous. Her protective instincts are fierce, and once someone becomes part of her circle, she defends them without hesitation.

Her speech can be slightly rougher or more direct than many characters, but it should never feel mindless or savage.
