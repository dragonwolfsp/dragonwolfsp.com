## Voice Direction

Lyra should sound grounded, emotionally layered, and increasingly strained under pressure. Her default tone is calm and compassionate, shaped by a healer's mindset. When conflict escalates, her voice should tighten rather than harden—controlled panic bleeding into determination.

She is not a warrior by nature, so moments of aggression should feel learned, not instinctive. When she chooses violence or direct action, it should feel consequential and internally resisted.

---

## Audition Script / Demo Lines

> "I can help you. Just stay still."

> "I don’t care what it costs me. I’m not losing anyone else."

> "If I fail you… then what was any of this for?"
