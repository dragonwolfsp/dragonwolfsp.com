Arazuchi

An aetherforged lion of earth and storm who welcomes strangers warmly and keeps faith with the few held closest.

Gender
Fluid
Pronouns
he/she/they
Species
Aetherforged Lion
Form
Hybrid Bioforged Liquiform Elemental

Name

Ara comes from the Japanese 荒, ara, wild or rough. It is also the start of arashi, 嵐, storm.

Zuchi comes from the Japanese 土, tsuchi, earth or soil. After another word it softens to zuchi, the same sound change that turns tsuki into zuki in mikazuki, the crescent moon.

Wild earth is the plain reading, or earth with a storm in it: the ground the storm walks on. It holds both of the elements Arazuchi is made of.

Temperament

Arazuchi is warm and open with strangers, and welcomes them before they have had to ask.

She is protective and caring both, watching over the people near her and looking after them.

He values quiet, deep conversation and small, close-knit groups, and he holds trust and loyalty very highly, most of all within those groups.

Anyone who proves unkind, or a threat, loses that warmth, and finds Arazuchi uninterested in them or openly hostile. Unnecessary cruelty towards someone who has not deserved it turns them coldest of all.

Arazuchi’s gender is fluid. He, she and they are all theirs, and this page moves between them.

Physical description

Arazuchi is warm from the first moment, and the welcome reaches a newcomer well before the size of him does. He is big and gentle with it, steady as solid ground underfoot, and safe to walk straight up to. Around him hangs a charged stillness, like the air in the moment before a storm breaks, and it settles over the people near him as protection, close and watchful without ever looming. Strangers are met openly and kindly. Those who prove unkind, or a threat, find that warmth gone, and in its place either a cool lack of interest or something far less comfortable. Cruelty towards anyone who has not earned it turns him coldest of all.

They are aetherforged and lion together, two creatures blended along one living spectrum, and they were forged long ago, in the age when the aetherforged were made. Wild magic came to them later. It settled the bioforged, liquiform and elemental forms on them and wove living biotechnology into their alloy until the two ran together into a single substance, and as it did, it gave them their colours, leaving none of the dark blue-black most aetherforged carry.

Arazuchi towers, and her size is seldom the same for long. She ranges across everything the lion and the aetherforged allow between them, and she usually rests near the middle of her spectrum, with both sides of her plain to see. She travels the whole length of it freely, with mood and with company, and the journey changes only her shape. Leaning towards the lion, she grows lower and drops onto four legs; leaning towards the aetherforged, she rises taller and more upright. Where her fur, alloy, crystal and every other part of her appears is wholly hers to choose, and it stays as she has set it wherever on the spectrum she goes. At every point her build keeps the same heart: deep through the chest and powerful through the shoulders in the way of a lion, solid and soft at the edges, with muscle that reads as metal that has grown there.

When he draws on his bioforged strength, his body answers by growing, and the swell builds with how much power he calls on. He can call that strength into each part of him as little or as much as he likes. Drawn through the whole of him, it fills out frame and muscle together, until an already towering body becomes larger and denser still with every proportion holding true. Drawn unevenly, it can leave him out of proportion wherever he chooses.

Four legs carry them, digitigrade like a cat’s, each ending in a broad, strong paw with thick pads and retractable claws. They drop easily onto all four, and they are just as easy upright on two, with the forelegs lifted clear. When they stand still they stand square and rooted, as though they have been set into the ground and the ground has closed around them.

Two sets of arms rise from her shoulders, and like every aetherforged she can twist the two together into a single pair or separate them into four. She moves between the two freely, without a thought for it. Her arms carry the same rainbow as her mane. Her hands are long and precise, almost delicate against the breadth of the rest of her, with slender metal fingers, paw pads across the palms and fingertips, and retractable claws of their own.

His merged form is his original shape, the one he always comes home to, and in it fur and living alloy share his body between them. Fur covers some regions and bare alloy covers the rest, and the alloy keeps to its own regions, never running beneath the fur. Beneath the fur he is living flesh, muscle and bone, with strips of crystal ore running through it. When his armour rises, it covers the furred regions and the bare alike. Coat and alloy carry the same colouring. Black is the ground of him, deep and matte, and through it wind slow spirals of red, green, blue and yellow, like coloured seams winding through dark, unpolished stone, turning across the whole of him, his face and belly included. The colour runs purest down his legs, where the black falls away: his front left leg is green, his front right red, his rear left yellow and his rear right blue, each a clear, pure colour from shoulder or hip down to the paw, fading at the top into the black with no hard edge. His tail and ears are a striking silver, bright against everything else.

Their mane is full, thick and heavy, settling around their neck and shoulders like earth that has lain undisturbed for a long time. In it the black vanishes, and the four colours mix freely into a near rainbow, in no set order, shading through one another along every strand. When power gathers in them, the mane lifts and stirs on its own, moving like a field before a storm.

Her face is leonine, broad and strong, with a full muzzle. From the top of her head rises a single pair of horns of pure crystal, earth-like and clear. They rise straight up, smooth along their length, to clean faceted points like a cut gem, catching every colour around them, with purple-white light held deep inside. The crystal reaches well beyond the horns. Strips of the same crystal ore run through her plates and beneath her fur, glinting wherever the coat parts, and some of them climb through the mane and up the neck to the head. There they gather at the base of the horns, thicken, and rise to form them.

His eyes glow from within, with no pupil, only the light. It is purple-white by default, and Arazuchi can shift it at will, to purple, to white, or to any of his body colours. His gaze is hard to look away from, and gentle all the same.

Their ears are round, a lion’s ears, furred in silver on the back and bare silver alloy inside, with silver tufts at the tips. They are the clearest sign of what Arazuchi is feeling. Their tail is a lion’s tail in shape and living metal in make, silver and finely segmented along its length, flexible enough to curl and coil wherever they like, and it ends in a dark tuft flecked with colour at its tip.

Runic script runs across her chest and shoulders and down her spine and the length of her tail. Under her fur the runes are formed in her living body itself, hidden until they light, and when Arazuchi works magic they glow up through the coat. Beneath the surface her energy channels carry the purple-white of her eyes, showing most at the joints and wherever the plating thins, and they pulse slowly and steadily, like a heartbeat in stone.

There is no seam anywhere in him. Born biotechnology and forged alloy have become one living substance, and nobody could say any longer which part of him came from which. The channels and the veins of the biotech run together as a single system, and the runes reach into all of it. Runes and channels both changed when the merge happened, and both carry the mark of it still. The alloy breathes, rising and falling with him, and keeps a pulse of its own. Arazuchi is ancient, as every aetherforged is, and he has never stopped changing: slowly, over long stretches of time, he is still growing.

Arazuchi is made of earth, through and through. Stone and crystal run through them beneath their plates and fur, and the crystal ore and the horns are that earth showing at the surface. It makes them heavy and dense, with a low centre of weight, and it hardens like stone whenever they brace or stand rooted. Through the crystal it glows faintly, like light inside a geode. It sheds no dust or grit; the earth in them stays theirs.

She is made of storm just as fully, and always. Lightning runs through her energy channels beside the purple-white, wind moves in her mane and fur when the air around her is still, and crackling plays across her plates and along the crystal. The storm comes forward most strongly when power gathers in her or when she fights, and then the stillness around her breaks open.

His liquiform and bioforged natures have run together so completely that he can choose what his body is made of. Any part of him can become fur, bare skin, bone, living machine or a pliant softness, and his elemental nature adds earth and storm to that. Each change can carry through the whole of him, and he has complete control over where each material appears. His plush form is fur, his sleek form is bare skin, his frame form is bone, his forged form is living machine from end to end, and his pliant form is a deep, yielding flexibility. His earth form and his storm form are each made wholly of his element. Every form can sit anywhere on his spectrum, and in every one his bioforged control chamber stays within him. His armour, blades, emitters, eye beams and wings go with him into every form, each taking on that form’s material, so that in his storm form his wings are cloud and lightning. Wherever his horns are no longer crystal, the crystal ore that runs through him vanishes with them.

In her plush form the metal vanishes entirely, and fur covers her from end to end, thick and lush, soft and warm to the touch. The segmentation of her body is gone with it, and beneath the coat she is mostly muscle and bone, with her bioforged strength still waiting there, ready to answer whenever she calls on it. Her spirals and the pure colours of her legs carry through the fur exactly as they lie in her merged form, dyed deep into it like colour worked into dark wool. Her mane is a full, thick lion’s mane of fur in its rainbow. Her ears and tail become silver fur, and her horns become bone of a striking purple.

In their sleek form fur gives way to smooth, bare skin, cool to the touch. The black of them warms to a dark brown, and their spirals broaden across it, splashed over the skin like paint. Their legs keep their pure colours, their ears and tail keep their silver as bare skin, and their horns become purple bone. Their mane is gone.

In his frame form he is bone, and where the black of him gives way the bone is a striking purple. His leg bones carry their pure colours, and his spirals are traced over the purple bone, both a little fainter here and harder to see at a distance. His horns are the same purple bone, and his tail becomes a chain of vertebrae extending from the spine, keeping its silver. His ears vanish, leaving openings in the skull. Purple-white light glows within his eye sockets, and everywhere else, between the bones and through every other opening, the light becomes a rainbow of his other colours, glowing outward through the places where his ears were. His mane is gone.

In her forged form she is smooth metal from end to end, segmented at the joints and along the tail, and seamless everywhere else. Her shape grows a little more streamlined, like a refined machine, and the metal carries a slight sheen. Her spirals and the pure colours of her legs lie across it as they do in her merged form. Her mane is gone, with smooth metal in its place. Her tail becomes segmented silver metal, her ears become silver metal, and her horns turn to silver metal as well.

Their pliant form is flexible and yielding, black from end to end and pliant inside and out. Its surface is soft, glossy and smooth, and it is thick, squishing deep under pressure before springing back. Their mane becomes a thick, flexible pad of the same material. Their spirals and leg colours darken until, in low light, they merge into the black, and wherever light catches the glossy surface, the spirals turn like colour in oil on dark water. Their ears and tail lose their silver and become a dark charcoal, very nearly black, and their tail grows more flexible still, as pliant as the rest of them, able to stretch and bend in ways it otherwise could not. Their horns are gone entirely.

In his earth form he grows more crystalline, over a base of basalt beneath, dark, dense and heavy. His colours become crystal and take on its reflectivity: garnet for his red, peridot for his green, aquamarine for his blue and topaz for his yellow. His crystal horns and ore stay exactly as they are. The pads of his paws become a soft mud that never stays stuck to anything he touches, only to him, and it feels lovely against the skin. His mane becomes that same soft mud, keeping its colours, and his tail becomes that mud too, in silver. He can soften the whole of his earth form into the same mud.

Her storm form is made of thick, dark cloud, with layers of coloured cloud and coloured lightning lying over it. All of her colours become coloured cloud with lightning running through it, and her mane is lighter cloud, forming its rainbow as it always does. Her horns are purple lightning, her tail is lightning in silver, and her ears are silver cloud.

Their eyes change with each form, and they glow purple-white in every one and can always shift. They are lightning in their storm form, crystal in their earth form, living eyes in their plush and sleek forms, machine in their forged form and pliant in their pliant form.

He can go fully liquid from any of his forms. When he does, the colours of his spirals and the base material of that form turn liquid together and merge. How much of his shape he holds depends on how far into liquid he chooses to go. Liquid, he pours through narrow gaps and around anything in his way, reworks his limbs into tools or weapons, and spreads himself wide to cover and shelter the people beside him.

Beneath her surface, in every form, light moves in slow currents and fine glowing veins. Its colour follows her eyes, purple-white by default and any of her body colours when the eyes shift. Feeling drives the currents, and so does gathering power, and they run in time with the slow pulse of the channels. The rippling every liquiform body carries spreads across her from the point where any change begins.

When they need it, their bioforged armour rises through fur and alloy alike in a smooth slide of plates, and the covering closes around it. Earth hardens over the plates as they settle and storm crackles across them, lenses close over their eyes and smaller plates lock along their face, until the whole of them is covered. The plates carry their colours, black with the spirals turning through them and the pure colours down the legs, and purple-white light runs along every seam, shifting whenever their eyes do.

His body holds a great deal more, concealed until it is called for. Blades extend from his forearms or his paws. Energy emitters sit in his palms and paws, glowing in the base colour of whatever form he is in. In each form whose base is black, every paw glows in its own leg’s colour, and his hands glow purple-white. Beams of energy come from his eyes in whatever colour the eyes hold. His senses reach far past the ordinary, in sight and hearing alike, and he can scan everything around him. When called, silver mechanical wings rise from his back, edged in purple-white light. The wings carry Arazuchi through the air, they can be thrown as bladed weapons, and they can be held up as shields over the people he is protecting. Within his belly is the living control chamber every bioforged being carries.

She cuts between realities with her claws or her tail.

Beneath the hand he is warm, like any living body.

Their feelings show first in their ears and tail, which turn, lift, flatten and sweep with every change of mood. Their fur and plates shift and bristle as feeling rises, and the earth or the storm in them comes forward, the ground steadying under them or the air beginning to stir.

She moves lightly for her size, with the long, easy grace of a big cat, and she moves towards people, closing the distance warmly. When it is called for, she goes from stillness to full speed in an instant, like a storm breaking.

He carries the earth affinity and the storm affinity, both brought by what he is made of. The ground steadies and answers under him, he shapes stone, soil and metal, and he feels movement through the earth wherever he stands. As a lion, he carries a tie to the sun as well.

The atmosphere around Arazuchi is warm and welcoming, like being let in from the cold. It is safe, like shelter in a storm, with solid ground underfoot, and quiet enough for real conversation to happen in it.

They smell of warm metal and fresh turned earth, of sun-warmed fur, and of ozone after lightning.

Arazuchi is a storm breaking over a mountain, and a hearth in a stone hall. She is lightning striking the ground and leaving glass behind, a rainbow over dark rock, molten metal poured into the earth, and a lion keeping watch over a sleeping pride.
