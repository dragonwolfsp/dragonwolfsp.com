Barqaram

A hypnanguil who carries a storm in her body and meets the world with the calm of deep water.

Gender
Female
Pronouns
she/her
Species
Hypnanguil

Name

Barq comes from the Persian برق, barq, lightning. In everyday Persian it is also the word for electricity.

Aram comes from the Persian آرام, ārām, calm, quiet or gentle.

Calm lightning is the plain reading, or lightning at rest. It holds both halves of her, the storm she carries in her body and the stillness she meets the world with.

Temperament

Barqaram is calm, with a steadiness that very little disturbs, and that calm stays the same whether she is playing, protecting or comforting.

She is warm and comforting, and patient, never in any hurry with the people around her.

She is playful too, though her playfulness comes out slowly, once someone is at ease with her.

She uses her hypnosis only with someone’s consent, or when she is hunting.

Physical description

Barqaram is large enough to startle at first sight, and then, almost at once, the startle settles, because nothing about her asks anyone to keep their distance. There is a quiet weight to her, like deep water, and the same calm runs through her whether she is enjoying herself, guarding someone or giving comfort. She invites people closer rather than holding them back, and while she seems entirely at ease, she is aware of everything moving through her field without ever seeming tense about it. Her playfulness surfaces slowly, once someone has settled beside her, and by then most people have already found that she feels like somewhere safe to rest.

She is about twenty-five feet long, thickly muscled and powerful all the way through, and her body keeps much of its thickness well down toward the tail, so that the strength in her runs nearly the whole of her length. Her muscle shows plainly beneath her skin whenever she moves or tightens a coil, and leaning against her is like leaning against something heavy and solid that happens to be warm.

Her skin is smooth and soft beneath a thick layer of warm, oily slime that gives her a constant soft gleam, wet or dry, and it stays warm to the touch even out of water. It is a pale silver that deepens along her back to a blue-grey, like deep water, and pales along her belly to a soft pearl with a faint warmth to it. Under the slime an iridescent sheen moves over all of her, catching violet and rose as she turns.

Bands of a paler, brighter silver run all the way round her body, evenly spaced from head to tail, though each band has its own width, some broad and some narrow, so that the pattern never quite repeats.

Her fin ribbon is the same silver as her skin, edged in a bright silver-white, and it ripples constantly, even when the rest of her is still. Her broad tail fin is especially long, trailing behind her through the water like a banner.

Her head is smooth, and her face is strong and clearly defined, with a strong jaw, full lips and heavy brows that carry a good deal of what she feels. Her eyes are set wide, a deep, clear sea green, and they glow that same sea green, softly and steadily, brightening only when she reaches for her hypnosis. Her gaze is warm and direct, and it holds someone’s attention easily, without any effort on her part. Her lips are a darker silver than her face, and her smile comes slowly and easily, widening until it shows the tips of her long fangs, with the inner jaw of her kind waiting further back. Behind her head, her gill slits are edged in the same silver-white as her fins, and when they close in air they show as faint, paler lines.

Her arms are heavily muscled, matching the rest of her, and long enough to reach well along her body. Her hands are broad and strong, with long, clawless fingers, and the webbing between them is paler than her skin and translucent, glowing faintly whenever light shines through it from behind. When her coils are holding someone, she will often rest a hand on them as well.

Her tail is fully prehensile, and she holds gently by default. Her coils feel warm, heavy and safe, and she curls them around someone to comfort or protect them, tightening into a true constriction only when she is hunting, protecting someone, or when someone prefers a tighter hold. She rests in loose loops, with part of her always curled near the people she cares for, and she will loop the end of her tail around someone’s ankle or wrist without thinking about it. Her broad tail fin often ends up tucked around someone’s shoulders, like a blanket drawn up.

Her feelings show first at the tip of her tail, which tells her mood before anything else does, and in her fins, which ripple faster or flare when something stirs her strongly.

When her field is on, a faint silver shimmer shows in the water or air around her, and the slime on her skin glitters faintly with it. Anyone inside feels a soft tingle, hair and fur lift a little, and the air near her carries the faint smell of a storm.

Under her hypnosis, someone feels as though they are floating, or slowly sinking, safe and held, while a soft tingling spreads through them. Sounds around them grow quiet and far away, their thoughts slow and soften, and their limbs grow heavy and easy, and the last thing they remember clearly is her sea-green eyes.

In water she moves slowly and without hurry, in long, rolling curves that carry her whole body as one, nearly silent and barely stirring the water around her. Her turns seem effortless for her size, and when she chooses, she can move with a startling speed. On land she moves smoothly and steadily, as though she were still swimming.

Being near Barqaram is like being wrapped in something warm before she has touched anyone at all. People feel noticed and listened to around her, as though nothing needs rushing, and invited to rest.

She smells of deep, cool sea air, and beneath it, of something warm and faintly sweet.

Barqaram is lightning seen from deep underwater, a calm sea at night with a storm far off, silver light moving through kelp, and a warm current in cold water. She is calm lightning, a storm carried quietly and held at rest.
