Bioforged

Living biotechnology grown into the body rather than fitted to it — and born, never made.

Overview

Bioforged beings are born.

They arrive the way anyone else does, already whole, and grow up carrying what they came with. The word invites a picture of assembly — a workshop, a maker, parts fitted to a creature that existed beforehand — and that picture is simply the wrong one.

What a bioforged being is born with is living biotechnology woven through the body, and a mutation carrying them well past the size their species ordinarily reaches.

The integration

The biotechnology is alive. It is body, of a different kind, and it behaves accordingly.

Mechanical structures flex and respond like muscle. Energy channels run beneath the skin the way blood vessels do. The systems breathe, shift and keep their own rhythm, and they grow, heal and adapt alongside everything else. A bioforged child develops as a whole rather than growing into hardware that was waiting for them.

Flesh and machine are continuous. Careful examination finds living tissue throughout and no seam anywhere, because the two were one thing from the start. It reads less like technology introduced into a creature than like life having taken another route to the same place.

Size

The mutation makes a bioforged being substantially larger than their species runs. How much larger varies, and it is conspicuous in all of them.

The whole body is built to that scale. Skeleton, musculature and the integrated systems alike are proportioned for the larger frame from the outset, so a bioforged being is sized rather than enlarged.

The living armour

Every bioforged being can bring armour up from beneath their covering.

It emerges in one continuous motion — overlapping plates rising through fur, scales or skin, settling into place along the contours of the body, and the covering closing neatly around them afterward. It is silent, and it is fast.

The plating is living anatomy. It flexes where the body flexes, restricts nothing, and belongs to the being wearing it as thoroughly as their own skeleton does. Colouring, coverage and how much of the body it takes in all vary by individual.

The control chamber

Within the body of every bioforged being is a living chamber, warm and organic, holding controls through which their body can be operated alongside them.

Consent is built into how it works rather than merely expected of those near it. The chamber opens to its holder alone, resists any attempt to force it, and can be sealed or emptied at any moment, for any reason or none.

Whoever is inside is a passenger with their hands on something, and nothing more. The bioforged being stays fully conscious and fully themselves throughout, working alongside whoever they have let in as an equal. Whether a bioforged being ever opens it to anyone at all is entirely their own business.

Other systems

Most bioforged beings carry integrated systems beyond the armour — capabilities grown into the body the same way everything else was, and concealed until they are called for.

What those systems are varies enormously, and it follows from the individual rather than from the form. A bioforged being with very few of them is as ordinary as one carrying a great many. This is a common feature of the form rather than a defining one.

Magic

Wild magic produced the bioforged mutation, both halves of it — the integration and the size. Its work was done at the point of making a body that grows this way, and the results are anatomy from there on.

The form settles how a body is built and stops there. Whatever a bioforged being’s species carries, whatever magic they hold, and who they turn out to be are all their own.

Field notes

Individual colouring, the extent and appearance of the armour, which systems a bioforged being carries, and whether they open the chamber to anyone all belong on their own member page.

Shared traits

Size
A mutation carrying them well past their species' ordinary scale
