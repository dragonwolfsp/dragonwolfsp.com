Hypnanguil

Long, powerful and Earth-born, carrying an electric field that can shock or take a mind under.

Content note. This entry describes mind control and a shock that can kill, and their use on prey. The Magic and Diet and hunting sections are the ones to skip.

Overview

Hypnanguils are long, powerful, eel-shaped beings. They were born on Earth, and live in the Veil, in the deepest parts of the oceans, or in both.

Every hypnanguil carries an electric field. It can shock, it lets them sense the living things around them, and it can carry magical hypnosis, which can take a mind under.

Physical traits

A grown hypnanguil is usually between twelve and thirty-five feet long. The body is thick and heavily muscled for its length, and powerful all the way through, the tail included.

Head and face

The head is long and blunt. Its front carries a humanoid face, expressive enough to show feeling clearly.

The mouth is wide, with soft lips that cover the teeth when it is closed. Behind the lips are long fangs. Further back in the throat sits a second, inner jaw with teeth of its own, which reaches forward to grip food and pull it in. A hypnanguil can swallow large prey whole.

Tube-like nostrils sit near the front of the face, and through them a hypnanguil smells keenly in water.

The eyes are large, made for the deep and the dark, and they glow faintly on their own. Eye colour varies from one hypnanguil to the next.

Gill slits sit behind the head, and close when a hypnanguil is in air.

Arms and hands

A hypnanguil has one pair of arms, set a little way behind the head. They are strong, and long enough to reach well along the body. When a hypnanguil swims, the arms fold flat against the body.

Each hand has five fingers, webbed between them. The palms are rough, and grip well through the slime.

The arms hold onto someone as surely as the coils do, and the two often work together.

Fins

A single long fin ribbon runs along the back, round the tail and along the belly. A small pair of fins sits just behind the head. The tail broadens and flattens toward its end, and it drives them through the water.

Tail and coils

The whole length of a hypnanguil can wrap around someone. Their coils grip and hold, and they can hold someone gently, with no squeezing at all, or constrict with real force. Which they do is their own choice.

The tail is fully prehensile.

Skin

The skin is smooth, and its colour varies widely. A thick layer of oily slime covers it. The slime is slick and warm, and pleasant to touch, not sticky. It protects the skin, and it makes a hypnanguil hard to hold unless they want to be held. It leaves no trail behind them.

Water and land

Hypnanguils breathe through their gills in water and through their lungs in air, and they are comfortable on land for as long as they like. There they move by curving the body along the ground, and pull themselves along with their arms.

Magic

Two things are fixed at the species level: the electricity in their bodies, and the magical hypnosis their field can carry.

The electric field

A hypnanguil’s body makes electricity, a great deal of it. That electricity is the work of wild magic, living in their bodies. How strong it is varies from one hypnanguil to the next.

Around them it forms a field. It reaches some distance through water, and a shorter distance out of water. A hypnanguil can widen or narrow it at will, or shut it off entirely.

The field is also one of their senses. Through it they feel movement, and the living things nearby. Hypnanguils feel one another’s fields.

The shock

Anything caught in the field is only shocked if the hypnanguil wants it to be. They can make the shock as light or as strong as they like. It lands as a sharp jolt, it can knock someone unconscious, and at full strength it can kill.

Magical hypnosis

The field can carry magical hypnosis, the same extra force a kitsune puts out as foxfire and a lamia delivers through the gaze. Here the channel is the electric field, and a hypnanguil reaches for it when they choose.

Once they do, it reaches anyone inside the field, whatever they can see or hear. The closer someone is, the stronger the hold. If a hypnanguil wraps their body around someone, that person is almost certainly theirs, unless their mind is truly exceptional, and direct touch is usually certain to take someone under. The hold lasts only while someone is inside the field or touching the hypnanguil.

The strength of it varies from one hypnanguil to the next. How, when and why they use it is their own.

Whatever else a hypnanguil is tied to magically is their own.

Diet and hunting

Hypnanguils eat meat and fish. They stun or entrance their prey before taking it.

Home

Some hypnanguils live in the Veil and some in the deepest parts of the oceans, and they travel freely between the two.

Young and growth

Young are born live, one or a few at a time. They are born looking very different from their parents, and change as they grow. They are raised wherever the family lives, usually by both parents.

A hypnanguil keeps growing for a long stretch of life, so an older hypnanguil is usually a longer one. When growth stops is individual. They do not die of old age.

Shared instincts

Hypnanguils share a comfort with touch and closeness.

They share no temperament. Who a hypnanguil is belongs to that hypnanguil alone, and is recorded on their own page.

Where the name comes from

The name is built from two roots.

Hypn is the Greek ὕπνος, hypnos, sleep, the root inside hypnosis. The hypnosphex carry the same root. It is there for the hold their field carries.

Anguil comes from the Latin anguilla, an eel.

Sleep-eel is the direct reading.

Field notes

Individual length, skin colouring, eye colour, how strong their electricity and hypnosis run, and how, when and why they use either belong on each member’s own page, along with whatever magic they carry beyond the species’ own.
