Metallurgist Dragon

Organic metal scales, and a control over metal that sits outside the elements entirely.

A breed of Dragon

What differs

Metallurgists are dragons with a deep control over metal, and they are built to match it.

Their scales and plating are organic metal, and they are stronger than the scales of any other known dragon. They run generally larger than other breeds, and their wings are larger too, extending further down the sides of the body than is usual.

Like the Venutians, they ended up with a homeworld of their own.

Magic

Metal control is an extra force, not one of the fundamental elemental affinities. That makes Metallurgists unusual among dragons, whose elements are otherwise ordinary fundamental affinities following bloodline.

Some Metallurgists carry an element as well as the metal control. Whether they do appears to depend on the individual rather than on anything about the breed.

Everything else is theirs alone.
