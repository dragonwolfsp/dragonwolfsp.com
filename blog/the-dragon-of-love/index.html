The Dragon of Love

He stood alone, unsure of the path.

His mind of stone, unknowing of his wrath.

All that he’d become, entrapped in chains.

Yet the One would come, to shatter his reins.

To take his hand, give him power to command.

Now he shall rise, beneath burning skies.

Spread his wings, soar into the light.

Unbound by strings, his name a burning might.

His roar the call, his fury the storm.

His strength shan’t fall, yet his story warm.

Darkness to fail, the dragon shall prevail.

His light unleashed, the One’s gentle beast.
