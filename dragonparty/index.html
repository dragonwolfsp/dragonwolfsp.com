New Year Voice Hangout

Hey folks,

Multiple platforms are available so you can join in whatever way works best for you.

How to Enter Voice Chat

  1. Join the server using the invite link above.
  2. Navigate to the Start Here category.
  3. Open the rules channel.
  4. Click the 👍 reaction on the rules message to unlock the server.
  5. Go to the Voice Channels category.
  6. Join the General voice channel.

Once inside, you’ll be able to hear and speak with others attending the gathering.

Conduct of the Gathering

This space is meant to be welcoming and enjoyable.

  • Discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.
  • Harassment, hostility, or deliberate disruption will result in removal.
  • This is a place to relax, connect, and enjoy the turning of the year—not a place for cruelty or posturing.

If you cannot respect others, this gathering is not for you.

Final Words

Come as you are. Stay as long as you wish. Speak, listen, or simply exist among fellow travelers as one year closes and another begins.

May your steps into the new year be steady.
